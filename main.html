Inception

A 2010 science fiction thriller directed by Christopher Nolan

Actors Quotes

Summary

Dom Cobb is a skilled thief who steals secrets by entering people's dreams. He is offered a chance to clear his criminal record and return to his children if he can do the opposite: plant an idea in a powerful businessman's mind, a process called inception. Cobb assembles a team and descends through layers of nested dreams, where time slows down and his own guilt threatens to ruin the mission.

Actors

  • Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb, the extractor leading the heist
  • Joseph Gordon-Levitt as Arthur, Cobb's point man and researcher
  • Elliot Page as Ariadne, the architect who designs the dream levels
  • Tom Hardy as Eames, the forger who impersonates people in dreams
  • Ken Watanabe as Saito, the businessman who hires the team
  • Cillian Murphy as Robert Fischer, the target of the inception

Favorite Quote

"You mustn't be afraid to dream a little bigger, darling."

Eames says this to Arthur in the middle of a tense mission. I love it because it captures the film's mix of ambition, humor, and confidence.
